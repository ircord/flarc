# IRC target budget selected for review

Authenticated IRC JOIN and PART requests share a durable account budget of 150 requested nonempty targets per 10-second window, starting with the first accepted reservation. Each command reserves its entire target list in AccountsDO SQLite before channel operations begin; concurrent devices, reconnects and Durable Object recreation share that account counter, and denial creates no partial target batch. Invalid or repeated requested targets still consume their requested cost. The existing per-connection command budget remains in force, including the ordinary one-target cost. JOIN 0 is exempt from the account target reservation so leave-all cleanup remains available, while normal disconnect cleanup consumes no target budget. This conservative account scope is selected for human review; it does not establish global channel capacity, retention or empty-channel cleanup policy, or a ceiling for native/provider WebSocket buffers.
# Ordered delivery during an authority outage

A failed channel outbox head is retained and retried by an alarm scheduled at least one second after the failure. This bounds retry rate; there is no lifetime attempt cap that would discard committed work. Later delivery in that channel waits behind the unresolved authority check, while independent channels remain usable. The outbox has a 10,000-intent capacity; failed retries do not append duplicate intents.

The persistent-failure regression observes four actual alarms over at least three seconds, one failing account lookup per alarm, unchanged pending SQLite rows and bytes, independent IRC/event delivery, and ordered recovery after the dependency returns. The affected recipient is disconnected and receives no queued private messages. Continued retry cost and the delay imposed on later delivery in that channel are explicit operational tradeoffs for review.
